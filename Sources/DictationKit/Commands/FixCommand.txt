How command mode works: the user speaks; a decision model (Jev, through OpenRouter) picks one command and one of its listed choices, from their descriptions alone; then the command runs with that choice and what was said ($COMMAND_SAID for a script). So a command can break in three places, and the runs above tell you which:
- it isn't picked, or another command is: its summary;
- the wrong choice is picked, or none fits: its choices' wording, or a choice that's missing;
- it runs and goes wrong: its code.

Do this:
1. Find the root cause. Reproduce it where you safely can, changing this Mac no more than the command itself would (prefer a choice that changes nothing, like volume +0). Don't guess: if the log and the runs don't explain it, find out.
2. Fix the cause, not the symptom, and check the fix the way the app runs the command.
{finish}

  osascript -e 'display notification "<that line>" with title "DictationApp: fix {name}"'
